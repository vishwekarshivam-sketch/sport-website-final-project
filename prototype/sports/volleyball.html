Spaces, equipment and practice.
Get to know what the sport offers.

Five Courts, Two Venues

  • 3 indoor courts at the Gymkhana Grounds and 2 outdoor courts at Old SAC.
  • Indoor courts give an all-weather training environment, ideal for competitive preparation; outdoor courts host regular practice and recreational matches. All courts are floodlit for evening training.

Equipment

  • Professional-grade volleyballs, nets, cones, and agility ladders.
  • Essential training equipment to support skill development and match preparation.

Coaching

  • Training conducted under the guidance of Coach Pritesh Yadav.
  • Focus on technical improvement, game strategy, and physical conditioning for both men’s and women’s teams.

Fitness & Support

  • Access to the institute’s gym and physiotherapy facilities.
  • Support for strength training, injury prevention, and rehabilitation.

Facility descriptions are from the institute’s October 2026 archive. Confirm current access, hours, equipment and coaching arrangements with the sports office.

Camps+

Regular training camps run for beginners and intermediate players under Coach Pritesh Yadav, covering technique, game strategy, and conditioning for both men’s and women’s teams.

NSO+

Part of the Government of India’s National Sports Organization scheme, under which all incoming first-year IIT students sign up for a sport and undergo structured training. Volleyball sessions run through the semester across the Gymkhana and Old SAC courts.

Badminton Court Booking Guidelines ● Only Court 6 and Court 7 from 4:00 PM to 9:30 PM are available for online booking. Bookings are to be made one day in advance through the IITB Sports website, under the “Gymkhana Bookings” section. All other badminton courts will remain available as per the regular court usage system (refer to the booked courts schedule on the notice board). ● Each booking slot shall be 30 minutes, with a maximum of 4 players per booking. No individual shall be permitted to book more than one slot per day. ● At the time of booking, the roll numbers of all players who will be using the court must be entered. Only the players listed under the booking will be permitted to play. ● The person who makes the booking must be present for the booked slot. Failure to do so will result in the person being banned from making future Gymkhana bookings. ● None of the players listed under one booking can make another court booking for the same day. ● All players must carry their valid physical Institute Identity Cards. The ID cards of all players present must be submitted at the reception desk, and all players must make an entry in the designated register before entering the court. ● Players must report at least 10 minutes before the commencement of their allotted slot to complete the verification and entry process. ● Players must strictly adhere to their allotted slot. No extension of the booking period will be permitted, and no requests for extension will be entertained. ● If only two players are present for a booking, half of the court shall remain available for walk-in players. ● If no player from a booking shows up for the booked slot, all players listed in that booking will be banned from making future bookings. ● Players must bring their own badminton racquets and shuttles. Equipment will not be provided by the facility. ● Players must be dressed in proper sports attire: boys must wear shorts and a T-shirt, while girls may wear shorts/full-length lowers and a T-shirt. ● Only proper non-marking gum-sole sports shoes suitable for badminton courts are permitted. Players without appropriate footwear will not be allowed to use the courts. ● The badminton courts are intended for use by students of IIT Bombay. All users are required to comply with these guidelines and instructions issued by the concerned authorities. ● The Institute Sports Council reserves the right to cancel or modify any booking for valid administrative, maintenance, or institute-related reasons. About this page.

The archived booking form and its complete source guidelines inform this page. Historical “available”, “requested” and “confirmed” labels are not reused as current slot states. The current external booking destination is preserved. This redesign has no reservation endpoint, identity service, duplicate-booking check, cancellation workflow or confirmation service. A future integration must verify slot freshness, capacity, eligibility and an atomic reservation response on the server. Local review never writes a booking. Source guidelines also describe a 15-day pilot; this page does not imply that pilot remains active.

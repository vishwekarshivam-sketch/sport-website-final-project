About this page.

The public archive includes only a logged-out certificate screen. Its component expects an authenticated /certificates/mine service and a parameterized /certificate-verification/:certId lookup; its fallback API origin is localhost:8000. Those settings and the source’s localStorage token behavior are not adopted. No sign-in, private document request, sample issued certificate, fabricated download or authenticity verdict is provided. Working authentication, authorization, issuer records, signed/document download access and public verification remain required integrations.

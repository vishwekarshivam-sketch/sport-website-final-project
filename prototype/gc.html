Reading the record.

The overall tables and discipline results reproduce the published 2025–26 data captured on 18 October 2026. Nine Boys’ groups and five Girls’ groups appear in the standings. Twenty-two Boys’ disciplines and seventeen Girls’ disciplines have supplied placements.

Overall totals are not recalculated from discipline points: the datasets do not fully reconcile. Group names, decimal scores, missing placements and tied points are kept intact. The separate attendance snapshot has no supplied season or event date and is not included in the championship totals.

The trophy and hostel setting form an original architectural illustration, not a reconstruction of specific IIT Bombay buildings or its actual championship trophy. The changing room lights are an artistic sequence, not a live occupancy display.

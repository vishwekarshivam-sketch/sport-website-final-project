# Rubik’s assets

All cube geometry, studio plane, lighting and registration lines are original procedural work in prototype/rubik-world-model.js. No downloaded 3D model, stock scene or generated people. Six classic cube colours are physical puzzle faces; supplied IIT artwork and Faktum files remain unchanged.

opening/finale × wide/portrait/compact/tablet WebP files are actual local Chrome captures of this exact 3D scene. The capture stylesheet hides every DOM element except the canvas and suppresses stage pseudo-elements. No title, nav, logo, call-to-action or status badge is baked into them. Eight responsive stills cover static, reduced-motion, no-WebGL and graphics-context-loss paths. They replace temporary vector development placeholders.

archive-1/2/3.webp derive from the three original Rubik’s gallery assets in the institutional archive (18 October 2026 capture). The archived compiled bundle maps uP/mP/fP to these images in the Rubik images array. See design/club-pages/rubik/photo-manifest.json for filenames, original SHA-256s, source URLs, captions and dimensions. EXIF orientation is applied, resize bounds 1400 px, WebP quality 86. Original files untouched. Existing marks within the original puzzle-display photo remain intact. Exact dates, events, participants and venues are unverified.

solved-diagram.svg is an original local vector diagram based on the final verified cube state. Artboard frames are original vectors establishing direction; they are not runtime scenery or a third-party asset.

asset-manifest.json records actual visual bytes and SHA-256 digests. Three.js reuses the existing local vendor module. The independent PyCuber QA library is installed only under /tmp/iitb-rubik-verification; it is not a site dependency.

01About this draft

These are descriptive notes for the local IIT Bombay Sports redesign, reviewed on 2 November 2026. They are not approved institutional terms and do not create a new booking agreement, licence or acceptance process. Final terms must be supplied or approved by the responsible institute team before publication.

02Using the sports information

The redesigned homepage, sports, clubs, Council, Events, General Championship and Yearbook pages are local experiences built from archived source material. They are not merely links to saved copies. Source dates and content limitations remain visible where relevant.

Schedules, appointments, contacts, eligibility and facility rules may have changed since capture on 18 October 2026. Confirm current arrangements with the appropriate sports representative. The Player Database is an archived directory, not an exhaustive current team roster; GC results and Yearbook material retain their own source seasons.

03Courts and bookings

This local site does not reserve a facility, take payment or confirm availability. A local court/session preference and its review are not a submitted booking request. Current booking links open the existing sports booking website; its current rules and service process must be checked there.

The complete archived badminton guidelines and turf guidelines are available for reference. Badminton source rules cover Courts 6 and 7, half-hour slots and up to four players. The turf rulebook’s one-hour maximum conflicts with two 90-minute source interface slots; those two preferences are disabled in local preparation pending clarification.

Identity, eligibility, capacity, duplicate bookings, cancellation decisions and final approval must be enforced by a verified operational service. Reading source rules or reviewing a local form establishes none of those checks.

04Reports, certificates and predictions

Feedback drafts and optional images stay local to the page. Intended recipients are drawn from the dated Council directory for planning only; nobody is notified here. Use a current contact route when you need to reach a person.

Certificate access is directed to the current issuing portal. There are no private certificate records, sample issued documents or working identity checks in this redesign. Preparing a verification link is not a valid/invalid certificate result.

Match prediction uses an explicitly unverified archived matchup for local score exploration. No vote is cast, no prediction is registered and no result is invented. Participation in an event is not created by browsing these pages.

05Marks, media and external links

IIT Bombay Sports and Bombay BlackCats marks and archived media are retained as part of this redesign. Their display here does not provide visitors with a new permission to reuse institutional marks, photographs or other source material.

External services operate separately. Their access requirements and published conditions apply when those services are used. This draft does not define or replace institutional rules for sports facilities.

06Before publication

The responsible institute team must confirm the operator, approved terms, eligibility and participation rules, booking and cancellation process, complaint route, media permissions and any other applicable institutional conditions. No jurisdiction, dispute process, liability clause or acceptance mechanism has been invented in this draft.

Operational services, approved rules and policy text must agree before launch. Privacy and Terms remain clearly marked draft and carry noindex metadata until that review is complete.

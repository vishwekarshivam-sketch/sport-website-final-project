Know the rules.

Original guidelines captured on 18 October 2026. These source rules may have changed; confirm them before making a reservation.

Read all archived turf guidelines
Turf Guidelines * Only turf shoes or regular sports shoes are permitted; studs are not allowed. Playing barefoot is also not allowed. * Individuals need to be in their proper sports attire i.e jersey and shorts along with the aforementioned footwear. * Avoid sitting against the turf net or leaning on the lower support poles. * The turf is designated for football only. Other sports require prior approval from the concerned authorities. * Please keep your bags and personal items outside the turf area and individuals who are not playing should stay outside the enclosure. * Turf slots can be reserved only 24 hours in advance, with a maximum booking of one hour slots. * In case of any advanced slot bookings for an event, kindly contact the Institute Football Secretary * This facility is intended to be used by the students and staff of IIT Bombay only and hence everyone is advised to carry their institute identity cards, authorities are allowed to conduct checks and cancel the slot if any violations are found. * The Secretary holds the authority to book and cancel slots in advance for any council events or for the institute football team practice slots. * The Institute Sports Council reserves the right to cancel any booking at any time for valid reasons.

About this page.

The archived booking form and its complete source guidelines inform this page. Historical “available”, “requested” and “confirmed” labels are not reused as current slot states. The current external booking destination is preserved. This redesign has no reservation endpoint, identity service, duplicate-booking check, cancellation workflow or confirmation service. A future integration must verify slot freshness, capacity, eligibility and an atomic reservation response on the server. Local review never writes a booking. The two 90-minute source slots conflict with the rulebook’s one-hour maximum and remain disabled in local preparation.

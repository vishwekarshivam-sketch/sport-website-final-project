Weightlifting
at IIT Bombay.

Weightlifting is a strength sport in which athletes attempt to lift heavy weights mounted on barbells in a series of highly technical movements — an Olympic discipline built on two lifts, the snatch and the clean and jerk, demanding explosive power, flexibility, balance, coordination, and mental focus. Led by Vijay Sonigra, a retired Nationals player and former Maharashtra Weightlifting Team coach, the club is a community of hardworking athletes united by one goal: getting stronger, pushing limits, and breaking records.

§ 01
 · FACILITIES
What's on offer.

State-level rods · Full machine stack · Coached daily.

Weightlifting Room
Many Nelco weightlifting rods up to state-level standards, plus powerlifting rods and platforms for training.
Fun fact: the room holds more weight plates than the Old SAC and New SAC gyms combined.
Coaching
A dedicated coach is present from 6 PM – 9 PM on weekdays.
Led by Vijay Sonigra, a retired Nationals weightlifter and former Maharashtra Weightlifting Team coach, with 20+ years coaching the club.
Machines
Lat pulldown, leg extension/curl, and leg press machines.
Forearm machine, hyperextension machine, and other accessory equipment.

NSO

Part of the Government of India’s National Sports Organization scheme, under which all incoming first-year IIT students sign up for a sport and undergo structured training. Weightlifting sessions run through the semester under Vijay Sonigra’s coaching, covering the snatch, the clean and jerk, and foundational strength work.
